# Week 4 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- [Stanford CS231n — Training Neural Networks](https://cs231n.github.io/neural-networks-3/) — learning-rate schedules, regularization, and diagnostics, at the level this week deepens.
- Andrej Karpathy — *A Recipe for Training Neural Networks* — the definitive debugging mindset, including the overfit-a-tiny-batch test.
- scikit-learn — *Classification metrics* and *GroupKFold/GroupShuffleSplit* user guides — precision/recall/F1, confusion matrices, and group-aware splitting.
- [PyTorch — Datasets & DataLoaders tutorial](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/data_tutorial.html) — custom `Dataset` patterns and `WeightedRandomSampler`.
- torchvision datasets & transforms docs — `ImageFolder`, samplers, and augmentation pipelines.
- [Guo et al. (2017) — On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599) — reliability diagrams, ECE, and temperature scaling, with reference implementations widely available.
- Loshchilov & Hutter — *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (AdamW)* and *SGDR (cosine annealing)* — the papers behind the optimizer/schedule defaults used this week.
- Lin et al. (2017) — *Focal Loss for Dense Object Detection* — the source for the focal-loss formulation used in the imbalance exercise.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
