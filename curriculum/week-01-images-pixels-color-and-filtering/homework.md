# Week 1 — Homework

Consolidate Week 1. A couple of focused hours; this sets up classical features next week.

## Tasks

- Write, in your own words, the difference between (H, W, C) and (N, C, H, W) and when each appears.
- Take one image and produce a labeled figure of it in RGB channels, grayscale, and HSV channels side by side.
- Extend your convolution to accept a stride argument and confirm stride 2 halves the output dimensions using the size formula.
- Read the OpenCV 'Getting Started with Images' and 'Changing Colorspaces' docs (in resources) and list three functions you'll reuse.

## Definition of done

A committed notebook or module implementing convolution from scratch with a small library of kernels (blur, sharpen, Sobel edges), a color-space demo (RGB/grayscale/HSV), and a gallery showing every filter on real images — with no reliance on `cv2.filter2D` for the core convolution.

Submit by committing your work to your course repo under `week-01/`.
