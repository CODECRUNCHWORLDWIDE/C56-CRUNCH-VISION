# Week 1 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- [OpenCV — Getting Started with Images & Changing Colorspaces](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d6/d00/tutorial_py_root.html) — official, hands-on; the practical companion to Lectures 1–2.
- Szeliski, *Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications* (2nd ed., free PDF), Ch. 2–3 — image formation and filtering, the primary text for this week.
- 3Blue1Brown — *But what is a convolution?* and *But what is the Fourier Transform?* (YouTube) — the clearest visual intuition for Lectures 3–4.
- [Pillow documentation](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/) — image loading, modes, and conversions; note the RGB-vs-BGR and dtype/range gotchas.
- [NumPy FFT documentation](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/routines.fft.html) — `fft2`, `fftshift`, and real-FFT routines used in the aliasing exercise and FFT-convolution challenge.
- Frédo Durand / MIT 6.815 Computational Photography notes — excellent treatments of gamma, color, and bilateral filtering, freely available.
- Wang et al., SSIM reference implementation and paper page — for the perceptual metric in the denoising challenge.
- d2l.ai / fast.ai preliminaries on image data — how images become normalized tensors for a network, bridging to Week 3.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
