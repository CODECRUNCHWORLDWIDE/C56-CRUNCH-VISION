# Week 1 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- [OpenCV — Getting Started with Images & Changing Colorspaces](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d6/d00/tutorial_py_root.html) — official, hands-on.
- Szeliski — *Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications* (free PDF), Ch. 2–3: image formation and filtering.
- 3Blue1Brown — *But what is a convolution?* (YouTube): the clearest visual intuition for the operation.
- [Pillow documentation](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/) — image loading, modes, and conversions.
- Fast.ai / d2l.ai preliminaries on image data — how images become tensors for a network.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
