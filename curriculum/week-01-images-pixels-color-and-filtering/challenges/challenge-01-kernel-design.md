# Challenge 1 — Design a kernel for a purpose

You now know that a kernel's *values* determine its effect. Design kernels to achieve specific goals and defend each choice.

1. Design a kernel that **emphasizes vertical edges** but ignores horizontal ones, and one that does the reverse. Show both on an image with clear vertical and horizontal structure (a building, a fence).
2. Design an **emboss** filter (a directional kernel that makes the image look like pressed metal) and explain geometrically why it produces that 3-D illusion.
3. Take a box blur and a Gaussian blur of similar size and compare them on a noisy image. Which leaves fewer artifacts? Why does weighting the center more help?

**Deliverable:** a note with each kernel, its output, and a paragraph explaining *why* those numbers produce that effect. The skill is reasoning from the numbers to the visual result, not copying kernels from a table.
