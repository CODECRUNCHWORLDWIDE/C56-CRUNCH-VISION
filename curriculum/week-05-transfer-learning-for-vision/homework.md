# Week 5 — Homework

Make transfer learning second nature — it's how most real vision is built.

## Tasks

- Draw the four-quadrant grid (data size × domain similarity) and write the recommended strategy in each cell.
- Explain, in writing, what catastrophic forgetting is and three ways to prevent it.
- Add discriminative learning rates to your mini-project fine-tuning and report any accuracy change.
- Read the CS231n transfer-learning notes and the torchvision models docs (in resources); list two backbones and their trade-offs.

## Definition of done

A committed project that adapts a pretrained backbone to a small custom multi-class dataset both as a frozen feature extractor and via fine-tuning, with matched preprocessing, augmentation, and early stopping, reporting a comparison of accuracy/time/overfitting, per-class metrics with a confusion matrix, and a justified recommendation of which strategy to ship.

Submit by committing your work to your course repo under `week-05/`.
