# Week 2 — Homework

Lock in the classical toolkit before CNNs start learning features for you.

## Tasks

- Write, in your own words, what each of Canny's five stages fixes about naive gradient thresholding.
- Explain the difference between a keypoint detector and a descriptor, with one example of each.
- Add RANSAC-based homography estimation to your matching notebook and report inliers vs. good matches.
- Read the OpenCV feature-matching tutorial (in resources) and summarize when to use ORB vs. SIFT.

## Definition of done

A committed notebook or script that, given two overlapping images, detects and describes keypoints, matches them with a ratio test, estimates a homography with RANSAC, and either aligns/overlays or stitches them — reporting keypoint counts, good-match counts, and inlier counts, with the matches visualized.

Submit by committing your work to your course repo under `week-02/`.
