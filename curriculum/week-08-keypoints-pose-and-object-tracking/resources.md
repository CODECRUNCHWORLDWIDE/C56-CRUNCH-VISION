# Week 8 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Bewley et al. (2016) — *SORT*; Wojke et al. (2017) — *Deep SORT*: the tracking-by-detection classics.
- Cao et al. — *OpenPose*: bottom-up multi-person pose with part-affinity fields.
- [torchvision keypoint & Mask R-CNN models](https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/models.html) and MediaPipe Pose docs.
- Luiten et al. (2020) — *HOTA: A Higher Order Metric for Evaluating Multi-Object Tracking*.
- First Principles of Computer Vision (YouTube) — tracking and the Kalman filter, intuitively.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
