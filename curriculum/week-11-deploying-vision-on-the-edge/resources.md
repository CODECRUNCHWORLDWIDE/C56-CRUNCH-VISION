# Week 11 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Howard et al. — *MobileNets* (v1–v3): depthwise-separable convolutions for mobile vision.
- Tan & Le (2019) — *EfficientNet*: compound scaling for accuracy per FLOP.
- [PyTorch — Quantization docs](https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/quantization.html) and the model optimization tutorials.
- [ONNX & ONNX Runtime docs](https://onnxruntime.ai/) — export and portable inference.
- Hinton et al. (2015) — *Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network*: the distillation idea. See also [C28 Crunch MLOps](../C28-CRUNCH-MLOPS/) for serving.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
