# Week 10 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Dosovitskiy et al. (2021) — *An Image is Worth 16x16 Words* (ViT): the original, and the data-hunger finding.
- Liu et al. (2021) — *Swin Transformer*: hierarchical, windowed attention for dense tasks.
- Liu et al. (2022) — *A ConvNet for the 2020s* (ConvNeXt): how much was architecture vs. training recipe.
- Touvron et al. (2021) — *DeiT*: data-efficient ViTs via distillation and augmentation.
- [timm (PyTorch Image Models)](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models) — pretrained ViTs, CNNs, and hybrids to compare.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
