# Week 10 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Dosovitskiy et al. (2021) — *An Image Is Worth 16x16 Words* (ViT): the original and the data-hunger finding. Read the appendix on mean attention distance.
- Vaswani et al. (2017) — *Attention Is All You Need*: scaled dot-product and multi-head attention; your C53 companion.
- Liu et al. (2021) — *Swin Transformer*: hierarchical, shifted-window attention for dense tasks.
- Liu et al. (2022) — *A ConvNet for the 2020s* (ConvNeXt): how much of the ViT win was the training recipe.
- He et al. (2022) — *Masked Autoencoders* (MAE) and Caron et al. (2021) — *DINO*: the two dominant self-supervised ViT recipes; read for pretext-objective design.
- [timm (PyTorch Image Models)](https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models) — pretrained ViTs, Swin, DeiT, ConvNeXt, and hybrids to compare under one API.
- [The Annotated Transformer (Harvard NLP)](https://nlp.seas.harvard.edu/annotated-transformer/) — a line-by-line, runnable implementation of attention; the clearest bridge from equations to code.
- Facebook Research DINO and MAE repositories — official code and pretrained checkpoints for the self-supervised probes in Challenge 3.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
