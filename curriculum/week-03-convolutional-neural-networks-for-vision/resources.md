# Week 3 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- [Stanford CS231n — Convolutional Neural Networks](https://cs231n.github.io/convolutional-networks/) — the canonical CNN notes.
- [PyTorch — Training a Classifier tutorial](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/blitz/cifar10_tutorial.html) — CIFAR-10 end to end.
- *Dive into Deep Learning* (d2l.ai) — CNN chapters: convolutions, pooling, and modern architectures.
- Zeiler & Fergus (2014) — *Visualizing and Understanding Convolutional Networks*: what feature maps mean.
- torchvision.transforms docs — the augmentation toolbox.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
