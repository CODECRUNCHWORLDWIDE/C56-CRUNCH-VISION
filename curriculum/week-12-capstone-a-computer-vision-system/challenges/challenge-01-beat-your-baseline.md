# Challenge 1 — Beat the baseline, provably

The capstone's minimum bar: beat your documented baseline on held-out images, and *prove* it wasn't luck.

1. Report your model and baseline metrics on the untouched test set, with the exact metric and threshold stated.
2. Show the improvement is real: report variability across a few seeds (or a confidence interval), not a single cherry-picked run.
3. If you *can't* beat the baseline, that's a legitimate outcome — analyze *why* (data too small, task too hard, annotations too noisy, wrong architecture) and document it honestly. A well-analyzed near-miss beats a fake win.

**Deliverable:** the head-to-head result with evidence it's robust to randomness, or an honest post-mortem. Scientific honesty about your own model is the skill being tested.
