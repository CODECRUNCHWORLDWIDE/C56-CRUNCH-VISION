# Challenge 2 — Demo and defend

Prepare to present your capstone as you would to a hiring panel.

1. Record or write a short **demo**: send a real image to your served model and show the prediction (label, boxes, or mask overlaid).
2. Prepare answers to the questions a reviewer *will* ask: Why this task and architecture? How do you know it's not overfitting or leaking? Where does it fail, and for whom (bias)? How did you verify preprocessing parity? What would you do with more data/compute?
3. Write a two-paragraph "defense" anticipating the toughest critique of your project — including any ethical or privacy concern — and answering it.

**Deliverable:** the demo plus the written defense. Being able to *defend* your choices — technically and ethically — is what turns a project into a job offer.
