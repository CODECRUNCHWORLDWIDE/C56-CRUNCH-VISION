# Week 12 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Google — *People + AI Guidebook* and *Model Cards* paper (Mitchell et al.): documenting models responsibly.
- Andrej Karpathy — *A Recipe for Training Neural Networks*: the diagnostic mindset for the capstone.
- *Made With ML* (madewithml.com): end-to-end project structure and MLOps for a portfolio project.
- Papers With Code — for vision baselines and metrics on common tasks and datasets.
- [C28 Crunch MLOps](../C28-CRUNCH-MLOPS/) to ship and monitor, and [C53 Crunch Nets](../C53-CRUNCH-NETS/) to deepen the architecture theory behind what you built.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
