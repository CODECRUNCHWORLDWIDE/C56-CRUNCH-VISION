# Week 12 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- [scikit-learn — Model evaluation & metrics](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html) — precision/recall/F1, confusion matrices, calibration curves, and Brier score, all with worked examples.
- [scikit-learn — Probability calibration](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html) — reliability diagrams, temperature/Platt/isotonic calibration; pair with Lecture 2.
- Google — *People + AI Guidebook* and the *Model Cards* toolkit — practical templates for documenting a model responsibly, including per-subgroup reporting.
- Andrej Karpathy — *A Recipe for Training Neural Networks* — the diagnostic, honesty-first mindset the capstone is graded on.
- *Made With ML* (madewithml.com) — end-to-end project structure, testing, and MLOps for a portfolio-grade vision system.
- Papers With Code — for vision baselines, metrics, and leaderboards on common tasks and datasets; use it to set a credible baseline bar.
- EU AI Act *Explorer* (artificialintelligenceact.eu) — a searchable, article-by-article reference for the prohibited and high-risk categories relevant to vision deployment.
- [C28 Crunch MLOps](../C28-CRUNCH-MLOPS/) to ship and monitor at scale, and [C53 Crunch Nets](../C53-CRUNCH-NETS/) to deepen the architecture theory behind what you built.

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
