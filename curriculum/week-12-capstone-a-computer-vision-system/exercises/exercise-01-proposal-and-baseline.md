# Exercise 1 — Write the proposal and baseline

**Goal:** lock scope before building.

## Tasks

1. Write a one-page proposal: the problem, why it matters, the task (classification/detection/segmentation), the dataset (and that you're allowed to use it — licenses, privacy, consent), the metric, and the baseline you'll beat.
2. Obtain (and if needed annotate) the data, and establish the **baseline** number on held-out images (trivial, classical, or a simple fine-tuned model).
3. Confirm beating — or meaningfully comparing to — the baseline is realistic within the week.

## Deliverable

The proposal and a working baseline with its metric on held-out data. If you can't get a baseline running, your scope is wrong — fix it now, not on day 6.
