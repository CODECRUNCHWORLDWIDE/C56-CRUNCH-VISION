# Week 9 — Homework

Cement motion and temporal modeling before Vision Transformers.

## Tasks

- Write the optical flow equation and explain the aperture problem in your own words.
- List the rungs of the temporal-modeling ladder and the cost each adds.
- Explain why splitting a video dataset by frame leaks, and how splitting by video fixes it.
- Read the RAFT paper abstract and a two-stream/I3D explainer (in resources); note how each handles motion.

## Definition of done

A committed project that estimates and visualizes optical flow on a clip (color-coded field), runs a pretrained action-recognition model on several clips with correct frame sampling, compares against a single-frame baseline to justify (or question) the temporal cost, and reports failure modes with an explicit privacy/ethics note.

Submit by committing your work to your course repo under `week-09/`.
