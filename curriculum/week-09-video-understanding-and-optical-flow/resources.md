# Week 9 — Resources

Curated, free where possible. You do not need all of these — pick what fits how you learn.

- Teed & Deng (2020) — *RAFT: Recurrent All-Pairs Field Transforms for Optical Flow*: the modern flow standard.
- Simonyan & Zisserman (2014) — *Two-Stream Convolutional Networks*; Carreira & Zisserman — *I3D*.
- [OpenCV — Optical Flow tutorial](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d4/dee/tutorial_optical_flow.html) — Lucas–Kanade and Farnebäck in code.
- Bertasius et al. (2021) — *TimeSformer*: is space-time attention all you need for video?
- [torchvision video classification models & the Kinetics dataset](https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/models.html#video-classification).

> All external links are references, not endorsements. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs) when they exist.
