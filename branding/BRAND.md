# C56 · Crunch Vision — Brand Guide

- **Accent:** Code Crunch teal `#14B8A6`. Teal sits between the analytic blue of data and the growing green of understanding — Crunch Vision is where raw pixels turn into meaning a machine can act on.
- **Voice:** pixel-first and visual. Never assert what a model sees — show it: display the image, the filtered image, the feature map, the boxes and masks drawn on top. Every claim is one you can render on your own screen. If a model detects something, you can point at what and say why.
- **Icon motif:** an aperture iris of concentric arcs narrowing to a single lit pixel — light resolving into meaning.
- **One-liner:** "Teach machines to see — from pixels and filters to detection, segmentation, tracking, and Vision Transformers."
